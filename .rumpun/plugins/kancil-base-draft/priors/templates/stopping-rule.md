# The stopping rule: what flips CONTINUE to EXHAUSTED

## What it is

The checkable threshold behind the usefulness assessment's verdict
vocabulary (CONTINUE | PAUSE | EXHAUSTED). The assessment is sealed at
every season close; the vocabulary alone names the verdicts without
naming the state that earns them. This prior names the state: exhaustion
is demonstrated against the ledger and the audit trail, never felt. It
exists so an EXHAUSTED close cites a threshold any reader can re-check,
and so CONTINUE stays the honest default while any front or candidate
remains.

## The threshold

EXHAUSTED requires every clause below, checked at the close in order:

- Empty fronts: the fronts list at this assessment is empty. Every prior
  front is resolved with its evidence leg or retired by name. A front
  that waits on the operator is not resolved; that state is PAUSE, and
  PAUSE is not EXHAUSTED.
- Zero candidates twice: the newest audit record names zero new
  actionable candidates, and the audit behind the previous assessment
  named zero too. Two consecutive candidate-free assessments; a campaign
  may raise the window, never lower it.
- No operator front moved: no operator-gated front had its next-action
  completed during the window. A completed front frees campaign work,
  which resets the count: after a reset, the two-window requirement
  starts over.
- Green floor: the suite floor is green at the close that seals
  EXHAUSTED. The campaign never stops over a red tree it can still fix.

The first clause that fails names the verdict: fronts present but all
operator-gated is PAUSE; any new candidate or any freed front is
CONTINUE.

## How to apply

- At every assessment close, check the clauses in order. The first
  failure names the verdict; the basis line cites the failing clause.
- An EXHAUSTED close cites every clause: the audit records it read, the
  front diff against the previous assessment, and the floor's exit. An
  uncited EXHAUSTED is a residual.
- CONTINUE is the default verdict. Absent a demonstrated threshold, the
  campaign continues by contract, not by mood.
- The threshold binds the campaign, never the operator: an operator may
  stop or redirect a live campaign at any time, regardless of verdict.
